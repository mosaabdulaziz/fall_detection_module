## Problem Addressed
- `[SOURCE: Section 1, Introduction; Table 1]` Identifies three bottlenecks of Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs) on coordinate sequences:
  1. **Sensitivity to pose-estimator noise:** Coordinate noise or missing joints significantly degrade GCN accuracy.
  2. **Poor multi-person scalability:** GCN FLOPs scale linearly with the number of persons $M$.
  3. **Difficulty fusing with RGB modalities:** Irregular graphs cannot be easily fused at early stages with regular 3D CNN grids.

## Method as Described by the Authors
- **2D Top-Down Pose Extraction Best Practices (`Section 3.1`):** Demonstrates that top-down 2D pose estimators (HRNet-w32 trained on COCO 17 keypoints) fed with GT/Faster-RCNN person boxes substantially outperform bottom-up OpenPose or 3D Kinect skeletons on action recognition benchmarks.
- **3D Heatmap Volume Representation (`Section 3.2, Eq. 1–2`):**
  - **Eq. 1 (Joint Heatmap):** For joint $k$ at `(x_k, y_k)` with confidence $c_k$,
    $$J_{k i j} = \exp\left(-\frac{(i - x_k)^2 + (j - y_k)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right) \cdot c_k$$
  - **Eq. 2 (Limb Heatmap):** For limb $l$ connecting joints $a_l$ and $b_l$, with pixel-to-segment distance $D((i, j), \text{seg}[a_l, b_l])$,
    $$L_{l i j} = \exp\left(-\frac{D((i, j), \text{seg}[a_l, b_l])^2}{2\sigma^2}\right) \cdot \min(c_{a_l}, c_{b_l})$$
  - **Subject-Centered Cropping & Uniform Sampling (`Section 3.2`):** Crops all frames to the smallest bounding box enclosing the person trajectory across the clip, resizes spatial dimensions to $H \times W$ (e.g., $56 \times 56$), and uniformly samples $T$ frames (e.g., $T = 48$) to form volume $\mathcal{V} \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times T \times H \times W}$ ($K = 17$ for COCO joints).
- **PoseConv3D & RGBPose-Conv3D (`Section 3.3`):** Feeds the 4D heatmap tensor into a 3D-CNN backbone (`SlowOnly-R50`, `C3D`, or `X3D`). Also introduces `ST-GCN++` / PYSKL improved GCN training practices in the accompanying codebase.

## Exact Reported Results (with Table Locators)
- **`[SOURCE: Table 2, Section 4.1]` PoseConv3D Backbone Variants on NTU60-XSub (Joint Heatmap):**
  - `SlowOnly`: **93.7%** Top-1, **15.9G** FLOPs, **2.0M** Params
  - `SlowOnly-HR`: **93.6%** Top-1, **73.0G** FLOPs, **8.0M** Params
  - `SlowOnly-wd`: **93.7%** Top-1, **54.9G** FLOPs, **7.9M** Params
  - `C3D`: **93.0%** Top-1, **25.2G** FLOPs, **6.9M** Params
  - `C3D-s`: **92.9%** Top-1, **16.8G** FLOPs, **3.4M** Params
  - `X3D`: **92.6%** Top-1, **1.1G** FLOPs, **531K** Params
  - `X3D-s`: **92.3%** Top-1, **0.6G** FLOPs, **241K** Params
- **`[SOURCE: Table 3 & Table 4, Section 4.2]` Robustness to Keypoint Dropping (FineGYM):**
  - When dropping 1 limb keypoint with probability $p = 1$, MS-G3D drops from **92.0%** ($p=0$) to **77.7%** ($-14.3$ pp), whereas `Pose-SlowOnly` drops from **92.4%** ($p=0$) to **91.5%** ($-0.9$ pp).
- **`[SOURCE: Table 8, Section 4.4 & configs/posec3d/README.md]` SOTA Benchmarks (PoseConv3D Joint+Limb):**
  - **NTU-60 XSub:** 93.6% (Joint), 92.5% (Limb), **94.1%** (Both)
  - **NTU-60 XView:** 96.5% (Joint), 95.8% (Limb), **97.1%** (Both)
  - **NTU-120 XSub:** 86.9% (Joint), 85.8% (Limb), **88.0%** (Both)
  - **NTU-120 XSet:** 88.9% (Joint), 87.8% (Limb), **90.1%** (Both)
- **`[SOURCE: configs/stgcn/README.md]` ST-GCN on NTU-60 XSub with COCO 17-Keypoint HRNet 2D Skeletons:**
  - Vanilla practice (80 epochs): Joint **85.7%**, Bone **85.8%**, Joint Motion **81.6%**, Bone Motion **83.9%**, 2-Stream **88.8%**, 4-Stream **90.1%**.
  - PYSKL practice (80 epochs): Joint **89.0%**, Bone **91.2%**, Joint Motion **86.7%**, Bone Motion **87.8%**, 2-Stream **92.0%**, 4-Stream **92.4%**.
