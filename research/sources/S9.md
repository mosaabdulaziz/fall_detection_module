## Problem Addressed
Automated, lightweight human fall detection for aging populations that can distinguish actual falls from confounding Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) such as bending, sitting down, lying down/sleeping, picking up objects, or exercising `[SOURCE: Abstract]`.

## Method as Described by the Authors
1. **Detection & Pose Extraction**: Integrates **YOLO12n** for human region detection and **MediaPipe** to extract human body pose landmarks from video sequences `[SOURCE: Abstract]`.
2. **Seven Posture and Motion Criteria**: Evaluates frames using seven spatiotemporal criteria derived from pose landmarks `[SOURCE: Abstract / Indexed Summary]`:
   - (1) Body angle (torso orientation relative to horizontal/vertical axis)
   - (2) Vertical drop (downward displacement of core landmarks)
   - (3) Height ratio (change in bounding box/skeleton height relative to upright baseline)
   - (4) Angle change (angular velocity/delta of joint angles across frames)
   - (5) Knee-ankle vertical distance (vertical separation between knee and ankle landmarks)
   - (6) Vertical velocity (descent speed)
   - (7) Head proximity to the ground (distance of head landmark to lower boundary/floor)
   - *Exact mathematical equations and numerical threshold constants for criteria 1–7*: `[UNKNOWN — not indexed in search_web; requires full PDF/XML download]`.
3. **Four False-Positive Override Rules**: Introduces four explicit rule-based override conditions to suppress false positives caused by ADLs `[SOURCE: Abstract]`:
   - **Head Position Override**: Suppresses false alarms when the head landmark position/trajectory remains elevated or controlled relative to the torso/ground (differentiating sitting/slouching/controlled bending from abrupt impact) `[SOURCE: Abstract / Indexed Summary]`.
   - **Aspect Ratio Override**: Evaluates the bounding box width-to-height aspect ratio transition to prevent non-fall horizontal postures (e.g., lying down deliberately or stretching) from triggering false alarms based solely on compressed height `[SOURCE: Abstract / Indexed Summary]`.
   - **Vertical Leg Override**: Checks the vertical orientation and configuration of the legs (hips, knees, ankles) to filter out upright/crouching/bending postures where the legs remain vertically supporting `[SOURCE: Abstract / Indexed Summary]`.
   - **Leg Alignment Override**: Examines the geometric alignment of the lower limbs to distinguish controlled sitting/floor exercises from the disordered lower-limb geometry of an accidental collapse `[SOURCE: Abstract / Indexed Summary]`.
   - *Exact boolean formulas and threshold numbers for the four overrides*: `[UNKNOWN — not indexed in search_web; requires full PDF/XML download]`.
4. **Temporal Validation**: Requires confirmation across **at least two consecutive fall frames** (`>= 2` consecutive frames) before confirming a fall event, preventing single-frame pose jitter or brief transitional bending from triggering a fall `[SOURCE: Abstract / Indexed Summary]`.
5. **Traceability Information Panel**: Incorporates a visual diagnostic panel that displays the real-time effects of the features and override rules on frame classification (Fall vs. ADL) `[SOURCE: Abstract]`.
