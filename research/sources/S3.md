## Problem Addressed
High computational complexity, environmental sensitivity, and low robustness in skeleton-based human fall recognition `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:2408.12211]`.

## Method & Stream Feature Formulations
The model processes skeleton sequences $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E})$ using a **Three-Stream Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network (ST-GCN)** with **Adaptive Feature Aggregation** and **Consecutive Separable Temporal Convolutional Networks (Sep-TCN)** `[SOURCE: Abstract & Methodology, arXiv:2408.12211]`:
1. **Stream 1 (Joint Skeleton Spatial-Temporal Stream)**:
   - Takes raw joint coordinates $X_{\text{joint}} \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times T \times V}$ across $T$ frames and $V$ joints `[SOURCE: Methodology, arXiv:2408.12211]`.
   - Applies Spatial Graph Convolution with an adaptive adjacency matrix $(A_{pK} + B_{pK})$ (where $A$ is the physical bone adjacency matrix and $B$ is a learnable adjacency matrix) followed by **Sep-TCN** (depthwise/pointwise separable temporal convolutions) to reduce parameter count `[SOURCE: Methodology, arXiv:2408.12211]`.
2. **Stream 2 (Joint Motion Spatial-Temporal Stream)**:
   - Computes first-order temporal motion (velocity) vectors between consecutive frames for each joint $j$ at frame $t$:
     $$M(j, t) = V(j, t + \Delta t) - V(j, t)$$
     (i.e. $X_{\text{motion}}(t, v) = X(t+1, v) - X(t, v)$) `[SOURCE: Methodology, arXiv:2408.12211]`.
   - Processes $X_{\text{motion}}$ through the same adaptive SGC + Sep-TCN blocks as Stream 1 `[SOURCE: Methodology, arXiv:2408.12211]`.
3. **Stream 3 (Residual Connection Stream)**:
   - Implements a skip/residual connection pathway $y = \mathcal{F}(x, \{W_i\}) + x$ (using $1 \times 1$ convolutions to match dimensions) to prevent information loss and gradient degradation across deep spatiotemporal layers `[SOURCE: Methodology, arXiv:2408.12211]`.

## Datasets, Metrics, and Reported Results
- **Datasets**: Evaluated on four benchmarks: **ImViA (Le2i)**, **UR-Fall (URFD)**, **Fall-UP**, and **FU-Kinect** `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:2408.12211]`.
- **Reported Accuracies (arXiv:2408.12211 v1 vs. Scientific Reports 2025 version)**:
  - **ImViA (Le2i)**: **99.51%** (arXiv) / **99.68%** (*Scientific Reports*) `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:2408.12211 & Scientific Reports]`
  - **UR-Fall**: **99.15%** (arXiv) / **98.97%** (*Scientific Reports*) `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:2408.12211 & Scientific Reports]`
  - **Fall-UP**: **99.79%** (arXiv) / **99.97%** (*Scientific Reports*) `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:2408.12211 & Scientific Reports]`
  - **FU-Kinect**: **99.85%** (arXiv) / **99.47%** (*Scientific Reports*) `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:2408.12211 & Scientific Reports]`
