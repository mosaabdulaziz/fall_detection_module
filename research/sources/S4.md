## Problem Addressed
- `[SOURCE: Abstract & README.md]` Existing skeleton-based fall detectors either use pairwise GCNs that miss multi-joint biomechanical coordination during ground impact or use Transformers/Dual-Hypergraphs with high computational complexity and latency, preventing real-time edge deployment.

## Verbatim-Verified Method & Results (`Abstract` & `README.md`)
1. **Architecture:** Combines a single-matrix hypergraph representation on **33 3D MediaPipe BlazePose joints** with **Mamba's selective state-space model** for impact fall detection `[SOURCE: Abstract & README.md]`.
2. **Verified UP-Fall Performance:** Achieves **95.13% accuracy** and **95.52% F1-score** on the Improved UP-Fall dataset `[SOURCE: Abstract & README.md]`.
3. **Verified Cross-Dataset Generalization (UMAFall):** Achieves **95.83% accuracy** in zero-shot transfer and **97.92% accuracy** in-domain on UMAFall `[SOURCE: Abstract & README.md]`.
4. **Verified Efficiency & Latency:** Requires **148.4 MB memory** and **11.2 ms inference latency**, achieving a **47.1% memory reduction, 71.3% fewer FLOPs, and 93.9% faster inference** vs. the dual hypergraph baseline, and **56.6% less memory, 85.6% fewer FLOPs, and 95.4% faster inference** vs. the Transformer baseline `[SOURCE: Abstract & README.md]`.
