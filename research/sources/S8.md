## Problem Addressed & Method
- Predicts human falls in advance from monocular video on the **Le2i dataset** (191 video clips) `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:1908.00275]`.
- Uses 4 stages: (1) Person detection, tracking, and 2D keypoint extraction; (2) **Keypoints vectorization** to strip global image position and background bias; (3) **Seq2Seq pose prediction** to forecast future keypoint trajectories; and (4) **Falls classifier** trained separately and jointly fine-tuned `[SOURCE: Abstract, arXiv:1908.00275]`.
