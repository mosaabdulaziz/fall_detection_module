## Problem Addressed
Low detection accuracy for small/distant human targets and weak multi-person applicability in human fall action recognition on resource-constrained edge devices `[SOURCE: Abstract, PMC11751301]`.

## Method as Described by the Authors (HFDMIA-Pose)
1. **Modified YOLOv8s Object Detector**:
   - Incorporates **SPD-Conv** (Space-to-Depth Convolution) to preserve fine-grained features of small objects `[SOURCE: Abstract, PMC11751301]`.
   - Adds a dedicated **small object detection layer** `[SOURCE: Abstract, PMC11751301]`.
   - Replaces the standard CIoU loss with **BCIOU** as the bounding box regression loss function `[SOURCE: Abstract, PMC11751301]`.
2. **Multi-Person Pose Estimation**:
   - Uses **AlphaPose** on the detected human bounding boxes to extract skeletal keypoints in multi-person scenes `[SOURCE: Abstract, PMC11751301]`.
3. **Two-Step Human State Judgment Rules (Skeletal + Kinematic Heuristics)**:
   - **Step 1 (Upper Torso Mid-Pendant Angle & Temporal Persistence)**: Computes the angle between the mid-pendant line (vertical plumbline) and the horizontal line of the **upper half of the human torso**. If this angle satisfies the fall threshold and persists over a time duration threshold $\Delta t$, an initial fall state is flagged `[SOURCE: Methodology summary, PMC11751301]`.
   - **Step 2 (Lower Torso Mid-Pendant Angle)**: If Step 1 is not conclusively met, computes the angle between the mid-pendant line and the horizontal line of the **lower half of the human torso** to distinguish bending over (where upper torso tilts but lower torso/legs remain upright) from a full-body fall `[SOURCE: Methodology summary, PMC11751301]`.
   - **Dynamic & Bounding-Box Features**: Combines instantaneous dynamic features (such as linear velocity/descent speed of the head or centroid keypoints over consecutive frames) with static posture features (such as bounding box aspect ratio width-to-height changes) `[SOURCE: Methodology summary, PMC11751301]`.

## Datasets, Metrics, and Reported Results
- **Datasets**: Evaluated on the public **Le2i** dataset and a custom **MPFDD** (Multi-Person Fall Detection Dataset) `[SOURCE: Abstract, PMC11751301]`.
- **Reported Quantitative Results**:
  - Compared to baseline **AlphaPose**, **HFDMIA-Pose** improves:
    - **Accuracy** by **+4.30%** `[SOURCE: Abstract / Results, PMC11751301]`
    - **F1-score** by **+4.57%** `[SOURCE: Abstract / Results, PMC11751301]`
    - **Processing speed (FPS)** by **+37.50%** `[SOURCE: Abstract / Results, PMC11751301]`
  - Compared to all baseline models on average, HFDMIA-Pose improves **Accuracy by 5.33%**, **F1-score by 5.51%**, and **FPS by 43.05%** `[SOURCE: Results summary, PMC11751301]`.
  - On the combined Le2i + MPFDD validation benchmark (Table 5), HFDMIA-Pose achieves **77.5% accuracy** compared to **69.9%** for CNN+LSTM `[SOURCE: Table 5 summary, PMC11751301]`.

## Stated Limitations & Conflicts with Other Sources
- **Limitations**: Even with modified YOLOv8s + AlphaPose and two-step upper/lower torso angle rules, overall accuracy on the combined multi-person Le2i + MPFDD benchmark is **77.5%** `[SOURCE: Table 5 summary, PMC11751301]`, showing that rule-based 2D pose heuristics degrade significantly in complex multi-person scenes compared to single-person clean benchmarks.
- **What this source does NOT establish**: Exact numerical angle/velocity/time thresholds and isolated URFD experimental numbers.
