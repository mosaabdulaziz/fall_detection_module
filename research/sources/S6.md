## Problem Addressed
Vision-based skeletal fall detection models suffer severe accuracy drops in real-world deployments when observing subjects from **novel, non-canonical rotated camera viewpoints** or under **partial body/joint occlusions** (e.g., furniture blocking limbs or self-occlusion during a fall), causing false alarms or missed falls `[SOURCE: Abstract, s25216783]`.

## Method as Described by the Authors (VIRA-GCN)
1. **Architecture — VIRA-GCN**: Proposes the **Viewpoint Invariant Robust Aggregation Graph Convolutional Network (VIRA-GCN)**, adapting a **Richly Activated GCN (RA-GCN / RA-GCNv2)** backbone for 3D skeletal fall detection `[SOURCE: Abstract, s25216783]`.
   - Multi-stream graph convolutional branches use activation maps to discover and extract features from unactivated/unoccluded skeletal joints when primary informative joints are occluded `[SOURCE: Methodology summary, s25216783]`.
2. **Skeleton Format Standardization**: Provides a mapping function to convert legacy Kinect skeletal data into modern **MMPose** (17-joint) formats for consistent cross-dataset benchmarking `[SOURCE: Abstract, s25216783]`.
3. **Dual Robustness Strategy**:
   - **Training-Time Data Augmentation**: Generates synthetic 3D viewpoint rotations and injects realistic partial joint occlusions into training sequences so the GCN learns viewpoint-invariant and occlusion-tolerant representations `[SOURCE: Abstract, s25216783]`.
   - **Inference-Time Aggregation**: Applies an efficient consensus-based prediction aggregation across multiple synthetic views at test time to stabilize predictions under extreme camera angles `[SOURCE: Abstract, s25216783]`.

## Datasets, Metrics, and Reported Results
- **Dataset**: Evaluated on the **Le2i** fall detection dataset (with simulated 3D rotations and occlusions) `[SOURCE: Abstract, s25216783]`.

## Failure Modes Highlighted & Relevance to Our Problem
- Demonstrates that **standard single-view skeletal fall detectors fail when camera viewpoints rotate away from canonical training angles or when key joints (e.g., hips, knees, ankles) are occluded** `[SOURCE: Abstract, s25216783]`.
- Directly challenges pure rule-based 2D keypoint overrides (such as `S9`'s Vertical Leg Override and Leg Alignment Override, or `S11`'s lower-torso angle): if lower-body keypoints are occluded by furniture or foreshortened by a high/rotated camera angle, rules relying on exact knee-ankle distances fail unless occlusion/viewpoint invariance is explicitly handled `[INFERENCE: combining S6's finding on joint occlusion/rotation failure with S9's reliance on knee-ankle vertical distance and leg alignment landmarks]`.
